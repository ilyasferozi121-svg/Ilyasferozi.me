01 / About

Academic foundations and a developing perspective.

I am an MPhil student in International Relations at the University of Management and Technology, Lahore. I hold a BS in International Relations from Lasbela University of Agriculture, Water and Marine Sciences, where my studies covered foreign policy, diplomacy, regional politics, security and international law. My background includes communications work in education, public-sector record management and mathematics teaching, alongside further training in research methodology and climate action.

Academic focus

My interests centre on how states define foreign-policy priorities, how institutions respond to shared challenges, and how regional cooperation can address security and environmental concerns.
